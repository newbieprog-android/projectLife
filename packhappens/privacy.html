Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Effective October 2, 2026. Applies to Packhappens.

About this policy

This policy describes how Packhappens, a travel packing app from Project Life by CV, handles information. It covers the Packhappens app; other Project Life products and websites may have different policies. You do not need a Packhappens account to use the app.

For privacy questions or requests, contact projectlifebycv@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Packhappens stores trip names, manually entered destinations, travel dates, bags, packing items, checklist progress, saved travel memories, and preferences on your device. This information is used to provide your checklists and suggest items for later trips.

Your packing lists and travel memories are not uploaded to a Packhappens server. There is no Packhappens cloud sync. In the browser version, this information is stored in that browser on that device.

Destinations and demo workspace

Destinations are entered manually and stay on your device. This release does not offer online destination search or weather forecasts, and does not request GPS access or track your live location. Destination coordinates saved by earlier development versions may remain locally until you delete those trips.

The optional demo workspace uses separate local sample data. Demo edits are cleared when you exit the demo; your normal trips and purchased access are not changed. Demo purchases are disabled.

Purchases and restoration

When in-app purchases are enabled, Packhappens uses RevenueCat and your app store to check, purchase, and restore Pro access. RevenueCat processes an app user identifier, purchase receipts or tokens, transaction history, entitlement status, and related app and device information for purchase validation and management. Entitlement checks may occur when the app starts, even before you make a purchase.

Your store processes payment details. Packhappens does not receive your full payment card details or send your packing lists to RevenueCat. Store and purchase records are separate from your local trip data.

Provider information: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy and https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/platform-resources/google-platform-resources/google-plays-data-safety. Your store also applies its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy or https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Fonts and other network requests

The app may download display fonts from Google when they are not already available on your device. Google receives your IP address and request information when this happens, but the font request does not contain your packing list. Google’s privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The current app does not include advertising or a separate behavioral analytics service. Purchase management providers may produce transaction analytics as part of their service.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid including passport numbers, payment details, or other sensitive information in a support message.

Retention and deletion

Local trip information remains until you delete it or the app’s storage is cleared. Delete a trip from Home to remove its trip, bags, and items. Saved travel memories are separate; remove them in Travel Memory or use Settings → Clear all data to remove all trips, bags, items, and memories. That action keeps your app preferences.

You can remove remaining local preferences by clearing app storage using your device or browser controls. Device or cloud backups may retain copies according to your operating system and backup settings. Deleting local data does not delete store transactions or provider logs.

Support correspondence and purchase records are kept as needed to resolve requests, maintain purchased access, and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us about records we control. Service providers apply their own retention policies to records they control.

Security and processing locations

The app uses HTTPS for its font and purchase services. Local trip data relies on your device’s storage protections; Packhappens does not add a separate encrypted vault. Protect your device and backups, and avoid storing passwords or identity document numbers in packing item names.

Online service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe their processing practices and safeguards. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can edit and delete your local information in the app and choose whether to use online features. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to ask about information we control. We cannot retrieve packing lists that exist only on your device.

Children and policy changes

Packhappens is intended for people aged 13 and older. It does not ask users to provide their age or create an account and is not directed to children under 13. If a child has sent personal information to us through support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal, subject to applicable law.

We will update this policy when the app’s practices change and identify the revised date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
