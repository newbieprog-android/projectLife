FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FreeDamn?

⌄

FreeDamn is a personal financial progression platform that helps you understand your financial position, track net worth, separate productive from destructive debt, manage the debt lifecycle, track settlement offers, and measure progress toward a personal Freedom Number. It is not just a budgeting app, a debt tracker, or a net-worth tracker — it is a system for knowing where you stand and what to do next.

What is a Freedom Number?

⌄

A Freedom Number is the amount of wealth or financial resources you determine you need to support your desired lifestyle without depending entirely on active employment. FreeDamn can estimate an illustrative target based on your lifestyle and assumptions, or you can simply set your own number yourself.

How is a Freedom Number calculated?

⌄

A common approach is annual desired expenses divided by an assumed safe withdrawal rate. For example, ₱720,000 in annual expenses divided by a 4% withdrawal rate gives an illustrative target of ₱18,000,000. Assumptions vary by person and circumstance, and this calculation is not personalized financial advice.

What is net worth?

⌄

Net worth is the value of everything you own minus everything you owe. Assets minus liabilities equals net worth. For example, if you have ₱500,000 in assets and ₱300,000 in liabilities, your net worth is ₱200,000. Net worth can be negative, and that is a starting point, not an identity.

What is good debt?

⌄

Productive debt is borrowing that may contribute to future income, assets, or wealth — such as a mortgage that builds equity, business financing, or education that increases earning potential. Debt is not automatically good simply because it is a mortgage or business loan. FreeDamn evaluates context, cost, and purpose.

What is bad debt?

⌄

Destructive debt generally consumes future income without creating meaningful financial value. High-interest credit cards, expensive personal loans, consumer debt, and payday-style debt are common examples. These debts can grow faster than the assets they fund, making them a priority for stabilization and reduction.

What happens when debt is defaulted?

⌄

Default means a borrower has failed to meet the required payment terms. The account may move into collections or other recovery processes depending on the creditor and applicable laws. FreeDamn helps you track status and evaluate options, but this is not legal advice — consider your obligations and professional guidance where appropriate.

What is debt settlement?

⌄

Debt settlement is an agreement where a creditor accepts an amount different from the outstanding balance to resolve the account — potentially for less than owed. Settlement can have consequences for credit, taxes, and future borrowing, so it should be evaluated carefully against affordability and applicable terms.

Can FreeDamn track debt settlement offers?

⌄

Yes. FreeDamn lets you record the original balance, current balance, settlement offer, actual settlement amount, offer expiration, status, and settlement date. For example, a ₱42,000 balance might receive a ₱14,000 settlement offer. The difference is not guaranteed savings — evaluate the offer, affordability, and consequences before acting.

Does FreeDamn tell users to stop paying their debts?

⌄

No. FreeDamn is designed to help you understand your situation and evaluate strategies. It does not automatically instruct you to stop paying creditors. Consider your contractual obligations, financial circumstances, applicable laws, and professional advice where appropriate before changing any payment behavior.

What is the FreeDamn Level?

⌄

The FreeDamn Level is a progression indicator representing your overall financial position and health. It considers multiple factors — net worth, debt health, financial stability, freedom progress, and momentum — rather than looking at net worth alone. Negative levels represent recovery stages, not personal failure.

Can someone with negative net worth use FreeDamn?

⌄

Yes. FreeDamn intentionally supports negative financial positions. Negative levels represent stages such as crisis, recovery, and stabilization rather than personal failure. Wherever you are financially, FreeDamn is designed to show you your position and your next move.

Is FreeDamn only for people with debt?

⌄

No. FreeDamn is designed for people across the financial spectrum. It can help someone recover from debt, build savings, grow investments, increase net worth, or work toward financial independence. Someone with zero debt and strong investments still has a Freedom Number to work toward.

Is FreeDamn a financial advisor?

⌄

No. FreeDamn is a financial tracking, education, and planning tool. It provides financial information and educational tools and does not provide individualized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
