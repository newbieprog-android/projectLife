keep it together — privacy policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

keep it together (KIT), by Project Life by CV, helps you track recurring responsibilities and keep local warranty, receipt and subscription records.

Records on your device

Your tracker names, categories, schedules, latest completion dates, profile name, receipt and warranty details, notes and attached photos are stored on your device. KIT does not provide a user account or cloud synchronization, and does not send these records to our servers. Device security protects access; KIT is not an encrypted document vault.

You can select or photograph an attachment using your device's system interfaces. KIT stores a copy locally. You can edit/delete records or reset app data from Settings. Uninstalling may remove local records. JSON export contains text records and local image references, not the image files. Protect backups and share them only with recipients you choose.

Purchases

Paid access is processed by Google Play or Apple and RevenueCat. Purchase receipts, transaction/subscription information, an anonymous app user identifier, and technical device/app information are processed to validate purchases and restore access. Store payment details are handled by the store; KIT does not receive card numbers. RevenueCat's policy: RevenueCat privacy policy. Store privacy practices also apply.

KIT contains no advertising SDK, location tracking, third-party behavioral analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not request access to contacts or device health databases. User-entered health-related reminders remain local like other trackers.

Sharing and retention

Export/share actions send the backup only to the destination you select. Purchase records may be retained by the stores and RevenueCat for billing, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Removing local app data does not cancel a subscription or erase records held by those services. Manage subscriptions in your store account.

Contact and requests

Contact Project Life by CV at projectlifebycv@gmail.com for questions or purchase-data requests. We may need purchase information to locate an anonymous transaction record. Do not send private receipt photos or medical information with a routine support request.
